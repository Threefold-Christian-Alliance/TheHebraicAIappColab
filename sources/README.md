# Sources

Catalogue of materials used to enrich or tune the Hebraic.ai harness.

Each entry should stay bibliographic and transparent (title, author/publisher, language, link or citation, status such as pending / admitted). Full copyrighted text belongs in the private ingest path, not as wholesale dumps here.

## Catalogue

### Studies in Pharisaism and the Gospels
- **Author:** Israel Abrahams (1858–1925)
- **Published:** First Series 1917; Second Series 1924. Cambridge University Press
- **Language:** English
- **Public-domain status:** Public domain in the US (published before 1931); marked "not in copyright" on archive.org
- **Source:** [First Series](https://archive.org/details/studiesinpharis01abrauoft), [Second Series](https://archive.org/details/studiesinpharis00abrauoft) (archive.org)
- **Status:** admitted (Oct 28, 2026)
- **Summary:** Abrahams was a Cambridge scholar of rabbinic literature. In these essays he reads the Gospels alongside the teachings of the rabbis, fairly and without polemics. He explains who the Pharisees were and how Jesus' words fit within the Jewish debates of his day. David Flusser and the Jerusalem School often built on his work, so the book helps Hebraic.ai give well-sourced answers about Jesus in his Jewish setting.

### Rabbinic Literature and Gospel Teachings
- **Author:** C. G. Montefiore (1858–1938)
- **Published:** 1930. Macmillan, London
- **Language:** English
- **Public-domain status:** Public domain in the US since January 1, 2026 (published 1930); the author died in 1938, so the work is also out of copyright under life-plus-70 rules. The archive.org copy carries no license label, so this status rests on the publication date.
- **Source:** [archive.org](https://archive.org/details/in.ernet.dli.2015.89751)
- **Status:** admitted (Oct 28, 2026)
- **Summary:** Montefiore goes passage by passage through the Gospels, especially the Sermon on the Mount, and sets beside each one the matching sayings from the rabbis. It is a practical reference for seeing where Jesus' teaching echoes, sharpens or departs from the Jewish teaching around him. This fits the "Jesus among the Sages" questions that Hebraic.ai users often ask.

### The Apocrypha and Pseudepigrapha of the Old Testament in English, Volume 2: Pseudepigrapha
- **Editor:** R. H. Charles (1855–1931)
- **Published:** 1913. Clarendon Press, Oxford
- **Language:** English (with some Greek)
- **Public-domain status:** Public domain in the US (published before 1931); marked "not in copyright" on archive.org
- **Source:** [archive.org](https://archive.org/details/apocryphapseudep02charuoft)
- **Status:** admitted (Oct 28, 2026)
- **Summary:** This is the classic English edition of Jewish writings from the Second Temple period, the centuries just before and during Jesus' time. It includes 1 Enoch, Jubilees, the Testaments of the Twelve Patriarchs and the Psalms of Solomon. Flusser and the Jerusalem School drew on these texts constantly to show the world of ideas Jesus and his first followers lived in. With this volume, Hebraic.ai can point readers to the original texts behind that background.

### The Words of Jesus Considered in the Light of Post-Biblical Jewish Writings and the Aramaic Language
- **Author:** Gustaf Dalman (1855–1941); authorised English version by D. M. Kay
- **Published:** 1902. I. Introduction and fundamental ideas. Edinburgh: T. & T. Clark
- **Language:** English (with Hebrew, Aramaic, and Greek)
- **Public-domain status:** Public domain in the US (published before 1931); marked "not in copyright" on archive.org
- **Source:** [archive.org text](https://archive.org/download/wordsofjesus00dalmuoft/wordsofjesus00dalmuoft_djvu.txt)
- **Status:** admitted (Nov 4, 2026)
- **Summary:** Dalman, professor of theology in Leipzig, asks how Jesus' words would have sounded to Jewish hearers, and sets them beside post-biblical Jewish writings and the Aramaic of the period. This is D. M. Kay's authorised English version. The English discussion and the transliterations are useful. Hebrew, Aramaic, and most Greek in this scan are garbled by OCR and are not reliable for quotations in the original scripts. The Messianic texts that form an appendix in the German volume are not included in this English edition.

### Judaism in the First Centuries of the Christian Era: The Age of the Tannaim
- **Author:** George Foot Moore (1851–1931)
- **Published:** Volumes 1 and 2, 1927. Cambridge: Harvard University Press. The Volume 2 scan is the second impression (November 1927). Volume 3 was not ingested.
- **Language:** English
- **Public-domain status:** Public domain in the US (published 1927, before 1931). Moore died in 1931, so the work is also out of copyright under life-plus-70 rules. These archive.org scans carry no "not in copyright" label; this status rests on the publication date.
- **Source:** [Volume 1 text](https://archive.org/download/judaisminfirstce01moor_0/judaisminfirstce01moor_0_djvu.txt), [Volume 2 text](https://archive.org/download/judaisminfirstce0000geor_g0o3/judaisminfirstce0000geor_g0o3_djvu.txt) (archive.org)
- **Status:** admitted (Nov 4, 2026)
- **Summary:** Moore presents Judaism in the age of the Tannaim, the teachers from the time of Herod to Judah the Patriarch, and lets that tradition speak through long quotations from the rabbinic sources. Volumes 1 and 2 of the Harvard edition are ingested. Volume 3 was not ingested.

### A History of the Jewish People in the Time of Jesus Christ, Division II: The Internal Condition of Palestine and of the Jewish People in the Time of Jesus Christ
- **Author:** Emil Schürer (1844–1910); English translation by Sophia Taylor and Peter Christie (d. 1905)
- **Published:** Division II, Volumes 1 and 2, 1910. Edinburgh: T. & T. Clark. Second and revised edition of a "Manual of the History of New Testament Times" (Clark's Foreign Theological Library, new series, vols. 23 and 24). The Volume 1 preface is signed Giessen, September 1886. Volume 3 was not ingested.
- **Language:** English (with some Greek)
- **Public-domain status:** Public domain in the US (published before 1931); marked "not in copyright" on archive.org
- **Source:** [Division II, Volume 1 text](https://archive.org/download/p2historyofjewishp01schuoft/p2historyofjewishp01schuoft_djvu.txt), [Division II, Volume 2 text](https://archive.org/download/p2historyofjewishp02schuoft/p2historyofjewishp02schuoft_djvu.txt) (archive.org)
- **Status:** admitted (Nov 4, 2026)
- **Summary:** These two volumes survey the inner life of Jewish Palestine in the time of Jesus: population and culture, the Sanhedrin and the high priests, the priesthood and the temple, and, in Volume 2, Pharisees and Sadducees, school and synagogue. Use them chiefly for institutions. Some of Schürer's views of the Pharisees are dated and sometimes negative, and should be balanced with Abrahams, Moore, and Montefiore in this catalogue. Volume 3 was not ingested.
